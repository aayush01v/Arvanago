It looks fishy because of a branding mismatch + basic/generic e-comm design that hundreds of low-trust Indian dropshipping-style stores use.  

Even though you're legit, the brain of a first-time visitor does this instantly:  
“EduSimulate? NEET/JEE simulations, 3D labs… wait, why am I suddenly buying an iQOO phone and JBL headphones?” → instant “this feels off” feeling.

### 1. Root Cause (Biggest Fishy Factor)
- Main site = serious education platform (NEET/JEE, 3D labs, mentorship).
- /store = random consumer electronics store with zero connection to education.
- Domain slug /store/nEy in cart looks like a temporary test link.
- No clear “Why EduSimulate Store?” messaging.

Fix priority #1: Make the store feel like an extension of EduSimulate, not a random shop.

### 2. Immediate Branding Fixes (Do These First)
- Hero banner on store homepage → “EduSimulate Store – Premium Tech for Future Doctors & Engineers” + student-focused tagline.
- Add a small banner: “Trusted by 50,000+ NEET/JEE aspirants” (use your main platform numbers).
- Create product bundles:
  - “NEET Prep Kit” → Tablet + stylus + 6-month premium simulation access.
  - “Gaming + Study Rig” → Phone + cooling pad + earbuds.
- Change store header to match main site colors/fonts exactly (right now it feels like two different companies).
