### **1. The Core Concepts**

* **Convergence (Settling Down):**
Imagine a ball bouncing. Each bounce is shorter than the last. Eventually, the ball settles at a specific spot. In math, this means the values get closer and closer to a single, finite number (called the **Limit**).
* **Divergence (Running Away or Wandering):**
Imagine a rocket flying into space (going to infinity) or a metronome ticking back and forth forever (oscillating). It never settles at one single spot.
